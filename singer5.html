Morad

Morad El Khattouti El Horami, known simply as Morad, is a Spanish-Moroccan rapper from Barcelona’s La Florida district. Known for his street-influenced lyrics and authentic storytelling, he’s become a voice for immigrant youth across Spain and the Arab world. His music mixes Spanish drill, trap, and North African rhythms, earning him millions of fans worldwide.
